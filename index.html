FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about online exam proctoring with ProctorAIQ.

What is ProctorAIQ?

ProctorAIQ is a browser-based online exam platform with AI-assisted proctoring. Institutions and recruitment teams can create exams, invite candidates, monitor sessions and review reports in one place.

How does AI-assisted proctoring work?

During an exam, supported webcam and browser signals such as tab switches or multiple faces are flagged as events. Authorised reviewers see these flags in the report and decide what action to take.

Does a flag prove a candidate cheated?

No. Automated flags are indicators for human review, not proof of misconduct. We recommend a clear review and escalation policy before using any proctoring tool for live exams.

Can candidates take exams on a mobile phone?

ProctorAIQ is browser-based and works on modern phones and laptops. Some controls, such as fullscreen enforcement or screen sharing, depend on browser support, so the platform skips them gracefully where a browser cannot provide them.

How much does ProctorAIQ cost?
