SECURITY & RESPONSIBLE USE

Built with privacy in mind

Security controls and policies should be configured for your organisation and reviewed before launch.

⌑

Protected data in transit

Use HTTPS/TLS and appropriately secured services for data transmission.

◈

Role-based access

Limit administrator and reviewer access according to job responsibilities.

▤

Audit-ready records

Maintain access and assessment records according to your retention policy.

♡

Privacy transparency

Inform candidates what data is collected, why it is used and how long it is retained.

Important: Automated flags are indicators for human review, not proof of misconduct. Configure consent, retention, access and escalation policies before using the platform for live exams.
